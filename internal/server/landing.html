parrot

{{.Domain}} is a parrot server. parrot exposes services on your computer to the internet over HTTPS or raw TCP.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/aelpxy/parrot/main/install.sh | sh
{{if .Anonymous}}export PARROT_SERVER={{.Domain}}{{else}}parrot login <token> --server {{.Domain}}{{end}}
parrot http 3000

Pages under *.{{.Domain}} are run by whoever opened the tunnel, not by this server's operator. Don't enter passwords or personal details on a tunnel you don't trust.
